# Privacy Policy

**Backend Automation Lab**  
**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Backend Automation Lab is a learning project focused on backend development, artificial intelligence, and workflow automation. This policy explains how information may be handled when using our LinkedIn automation project.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled, the project may process information you provide, LinkedIn profile information made available through authorized LinkedIn APIs, post content, workflow execution data, and authentication tokens required to connect services.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to draft, review, validate, and publish LinkedIn content when explicitly authorized. It may also be used to troubleshoot workflow errors and improve the learning project.

## 4. AI Services and Third Parties

If an AI provider is configured in the workflow, content submitted to that provider may be processed under that provider's applicable terms and privacy policy. Information is not intentionally shared with other services except where needed to operate configured integrations or where required by law.

## 5. Storage and Security

The project aims to limit stored information to what is necessary for its operation. Credentials and access tokens should be stored securely and must not be published in source code or public repositories. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 6. LinkedIn Authorization and Control

The project uses LinkedIn integrations only when authorized by the account holder and subject to LinkedIn's applicable terms and permissions. Users should review proposed content before publication and may revoke the application's access through LinkedIn account settings.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the project's intended operation, troubleshooting, or legal obligations. Requests concerning personal information or deletion can be directed to the contact below.

## 8. Children's Privacy

This learning project is not intended to collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the project develops. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

## 10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the project maintainer through the GitHub profile associated with Backend Automation Lab.
